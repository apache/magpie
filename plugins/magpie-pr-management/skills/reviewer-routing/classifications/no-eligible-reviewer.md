# NO ELIGIBLE REVIEWER

`reviewer-routing propose` returned `no_eligible_reviewer: true`: the roster is empty or unresolved, or every eligible member is OVERLOADED.
Print the proposal as-is and say it needs a maintainer call.
Never fill the gap with a handle from git history or CODEOWNERS alone — every suggestion must be a roster member.
